> **Little Angel to Good Hearts, Little Devil to Bad Ones.**

To kind people it is a little angel. To bad ones it becomes a frightening little devil.

## Cloud Hex Star

Six rounded points. Somewhere between a cloud, a mochi and a starfish — not quite symmetrical, soft and round, drawn with the hand of an 11-year-old.

**It is not a star. It is a dream creature.**

## Character DNA

| | |
|---|---|
| Shape | Cloud hex star — six rounded points, deliberately imperfect |
| Face | Very wide-set eyes `• •` and an `X` mouth |
| Wings | A pair of angel wings, always present — they make the three-view silhouette recognisable |
| Horns | Two small devil horns |
| Tail | A hidden devil tail with magic in it. It only appears in front of bad people |

The wings are the constant. The tail is the condition.
