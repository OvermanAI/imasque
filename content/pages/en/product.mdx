The first collection takes the silhouette of sunglasses and turns it soft. A two-tone mask you wear to step out of the day — not a medical accessory, but a small object of escape.

## Status

**In the making.** Not for sale yet.

We're building it in the open — every colour choice and every wrong turn gets written down in the [journal](/journal) as it happens.

## Where the shape came from

Borrowed from sunglasses. The outline is familiar; the material is not. Soft where sunglasses are hard, quiet where they are a statement.
