IMASQUE is being built in the open by CxY. That means the unfinished parts are published too.

## Where it stands

The first piece — the Sunglasses Mask — is in the making. It is not for sale. There is no factory confirmed and no material qualified yet.

Those fields stay empty until they are true.

| Field | Status |
|---|---|
| Materials | Pending |
| Factories | Pending |
| MOQ | Pending |
| Cost breakdown | Pending |

The same information is published as machine-readable data at [`/api/brand.json`](/api/brand.json).

## Why publish it empty

Because writing "coming soon" over a gap is a way of hiding it. An empty field is a true statement about where the brand actually is — and it is the part that decides whether the rest means anything.
