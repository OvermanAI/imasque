IMASQUE started as a question an 11-year-old asked out loud: what if an eye mask didn't have to look like an eye mask?

Right now the answer exists as a shape — the silhouette of a pair of sunglasses, made soft. It exists as a character, Star Monster. It exists as three words the brand keeps coming back to: comfort, healing, imagination.

What it does not exist as yet is an object you can hold.

## Why write it down now

The easy version of a brand launch is to appear when everything is finished — the fabric chosen, the factory confirmed, the photographs taken. Nobody sees the two years of not knowing.

This one is being written from the other end. The colour choices, the wrong turns, the small wins go into this journal as they happen, including the parts where the honest answer is "we don't know yet".

## What comes next

The first piece is in the making. The [materials page](/transparency) shows exactly how much of it is still undecided — the empty fields are the point, not an oversight.
