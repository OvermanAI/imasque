# imasque Homepage Hit Product Design

Date: 2026-06-25
Status: Planning only. Do not implement from this file until the homepage direction is approved.

## Core Direction

The homepage should not feel like a simple product introduction.

It should make visitors understand, within 5 seconds:

1. What is imasque?
2. What is the product?
3. Why would this sleep mask become a hit product?

The homepage should not say, "imasque has a chance to become a hit."

It should make visitors think:

> No wonder this became a hit.

## Brand Priority

imasque is the main character.

The product is the main visual object.

CxY is the creative origin and final trust-builder.

StarMonster is a brand-world character and visual accent, not the brand logo and not the main homepage subject.

Recommended homepage weight:

- Product and imasque brand: 70%
- CxY: 20%
- StarMonster and brand-world elements: 10%

## One-Sentence Positioning

English:

> The $10 sleep mask people actually want to show.

Traditional Chinese:

## Five Hit-Product Conversion Levers

### 1. Easy to Buy

Core idea:

> $10 lowers the buying threshold.

Homepage feeling:

This is not a high-pressure purchase. It is cute, memorable, giftable, and easy to collect.

Messaging:

- $10. Easy to buy. Easy to gift. Easy to collect.
- 10 美金，剛好是不用想太久的可愛。
- For you. For a friend. For your plushie.

### 2. Easy to Show

Core idea:

> imasque is not only used. It is shown.

Homepage feeling:

Visitors should imagine taking a photo the moment they see someone wearing it.

Messaging:

- It looks like sunglasses. It is made for sleep.
- Not every sleep mask makes people want to take a selfie.
- 戴起來又酷、又有趣。

### 3. Ritual

Core idea:

> Wearing imasque means entering a specific state.

Ritual should not only mean bedtime. It should include multiple high-conversion use cases:

- CxY and her favorite plushies getting ready for sleep
- A stylish girl resting on a plane
- A boy entering recharge mode in a dream gaming room
- A group of young girls taking selfies together

Messaging:

- Put it on. Close your eyes. Start the dream.
- Airport fit. In-flight sleep. One mask.
- Pit stop for your brain.
- The sleep mask made for selfies.

### 4. Everyone Is Wearing It

Core idea:

> After launch, UGC becomes proof that the product is really spreading.

Pre-launch version:

Use AI-generated images as CxY's imagined application scenes. Do not pretend they are real customer photos.

Section name:

> CxY's AI Moodboard

Pre-launch explanation:

> Before imasque appears in real life, CxY used AI to imagine where it could go.

Traditional Chinese:

> 在 imasque 真正出現在大家生活前，CxY 先把她想像中的使用場景，用 AI 做出來。

Required label:

> Imagined by CxY, generated with AI. Not real customer photos.

After launch:

Replace this section with real UGC videos and photos.

Future section name:

> Real Dreamers Gallery

### 5. Remove Purchase Resistance

Core idea:

> The final trust-builder should come from CxY, not from corporate FAQ language.

This section should be called:

> CxY's Promise

It should remove concerns about comfort, quality, product guarantee, shipping, returns, and gifting, but in CxY's sincere voice.

Tone:

- Honest
- Childlike but clear
- Warm
- Not corporate
- Not exaggerated

Draft copy:

> 我不想做一個只是看起來很酷，  
> 但戴起來不舒服的眼罩。  
>
> 我希望 imasque 是軟軟的、好戴的，  
> 睡覺前、搭飛機、想休息的時候，  
> 戴上都會覺得很開心。  
>
> 如果你是買給自己、朋友，  
> 或你的玩偶，  
> 我希望你打開它的時候會覺得：  
>
> 今晚好像可以做一個很可愛的夢。  
>
> 如果產品有問題，  
> 我們會好好處理。  
>
> 因為這不是一個普通眼罩，  
> 這是我第一個真的想做出來的夢。  
>
> Dream More,  
> CxY

## Required Image Set

Use 8 key images first:

- 4 product images
- 4 ritual/application images

The goal is not to fill the homepage with many images. The goal is to make each image answer a conversion question.

### Product Images

#### Product Image 1: Hero Product Visual

Purpose:

Create instant understanding that imasque is a sleep mask designed like sunglasses.

Visual:

- Single imasque product as the main object
- Photographed like sunglasses or a fashion accessory
- Clean, bold, high-contrast composition
- Product shape must be unmistakable

Homepage job:

Answer: What is the product?

#### Product Image 2: Shape and Two-Color Detail

Purpose:

Show that the product has a strong design language, not a generic sleep mask shape.

Visual:

- Clear front or 45-degree angle
- Show two-color blocking
- Show sunglasses-like frame outline
- Sharp enough to understand product construction

Homepage job:

Answer: Why is it memorable?

#### Product Image 3: Soft Material Detail

Purpose:

Remove the concern that the product only looks good but may not be comfortable.

Visual:

- Close-up of fabric
- Inner side
- Edge details
- Strap or fit detail
- Softness should be visible

Homepage job:

Answer: Will it feel good?

#### Product Image 4: Packaging and $10 Giftable Moment

Purpose:

Make the product feel easy to buy, easy to gift, and easy to collect.

Visual:

- Product with packaging
- Price tag or graphic cue: "$10"
- Giftable styling
- Optional multiple colors or first drop hint

Homepage job:

Answer: Why is it easy to buy?

### Ritual / Application Images

#### Ritual Image 1: Brand Soul - CxY and Plushies at Bedtime

Purpose:

Create emotional origin, bedtime ritual, and founder trust.

Visual:

- CxY wearing imasque
- Favorite plushies also wearing mini imasque masks
- Bedtime setting
- Soft light
- Warm, real, not overly polished

Homepage job:

Answer: Why does this brand feel real and lovable?

Messaging:

- The mask should match clothing and accessories

Homepage job:

Messaging:

Answer: Can imasque become a product series for different identities?

Messaging:

- Pit stop for your brain.
- 戴上 F1 imasque，進入夢幻補給站。

#### Ritual Image 4: Young Girls Selfie Moment

Purpose:

Prove "easy to show" and social sharing potential.

Visual:

- A group of young girls wearing imasque

- Sleepover, travel stay, or party mood

- The photo should feel like something users would post

Homepage job:

Answer: Why would people share this?

Messaging:

- The sleep mask made for selfies.
- 不是每個眼罩，都會讓朋友一起戴上拍照。

## Visual Atmosphere

The homepage should feel:

- Product-first
- Youthful
- Fashionable
- High-energy
- Easy to screenshot
- Cute but not childish
- Playful but not messy
- Like a product people are already starting to talk about

Avoid:

- Making CxY the only main subject
- Making StarMonster the logo or primary product replacement
- Too much lore before the product is understood
- A page that feels like a character IP site
- Fake UGC or fake testimonials
- Corporate FAQ tone in the final trust section

## Success Criteria

The homepage succeeds if a visitor can say:

1. imasque is a sleep mask brand.
2. The product is a $10 sleep mask that looks like sunglasses.
3. It can become a hit because it is easy to buy, fun to show, useful across rituals, socially shareable, and emotionally trusted through CxY.

The target visitor reaction:

> 難怪這個眼罩會紅。
